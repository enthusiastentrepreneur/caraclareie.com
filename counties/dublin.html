Support for older people & family carers in Dublin

Dublin is Ireland's biggest county by far, split across four local authorities — Dublin City, South Dublin, Fingal, and Dún Laoghaire-Rathdown. Isolation among older people is rising fastest in the newer suburbs: living-alone rates jumped 36% in Fingal and 33% in South Dublin since 2016. Here's real, checked local support.

Why Dublin needs this now

195,664 people 65+ — up 19% since 2016
47,578 of them live alone, across Dublin's 4 local authorities
76,100 unpaid family carers — 5% of the population, up from 4%
21,086 now give 43+ hrs/week care, up from 9,944 in 2016

Source: CSO Census of Population 2022, Dublin county profiles.

Family carers

Family Carers Ireland — Dublin

Multiple Dublin support centres

Dublin has several local support centres (including Dublin South); the national office helps you find the nearest one, with a dedicated carer support manager, groups, and training.

1800 24 07 24 Find your nearest centre Across Dublin
Transport

Dublin Bus — Independent Travel Support

Free one-to-one travel training

A trained team member travels your regular route with you until you feel confident using Dublin Bus, Luas, or DART independently. Free Travel pass holders travel at no cost on all three.

01 703 3002 dublinbus.ie Greater Dublin Area
